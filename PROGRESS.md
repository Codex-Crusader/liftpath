## Method issues
- Phase B: The liftpath_explore track swings between cohorts. Probable cause: in both worlds, cohorts that exploit quiz (82% of advice) reach high pass rates, so one more quiz week adds less (sigmoid saturation), and the pooled Wald range for quiz then touches 0. This cause is not checked per cohort. Every action has an available experimental effect, so exploit gives no advice in the next cohort (advice only from the 20% explore share). That cohort gives a larger effect again. Standard world pass rates: 49.3%, 63.8%, 47.7%, 62.4%, 48.4% (cohorts 1 to 5). Hidden world: 49.0%, 45.9%, 66.1%, 47.4%, 61.0%. The final cohort of the standard world (48.4%) is below liftpath (63.7%). The experimental quiz estimates of the explore track (+5 to +24 pp) are above the true effect (+4 to +5 pp) in all 8 cohorts. 7 of 8 ranges hold the true effect. The standard world cohort 2 range (+5.2 to +40.0 pp) does not (true +4.2 pp).
- Phase B check: under the random policy (every decision randomized, cohorts 1 to 5, 1,000 students each), the Wald quiz effect matches the truth. Config seed: standard 0.050 (range 0.017 to 0.084, true 0.051), hidden 0.087 (0.057 to 0.118, true 0.050). Seeds 1 to 4: 0.038 to 0.076 against true 0.051 to 0.052. 9 of 10 ranges hold the truth, and the mean of the 10 estimates is 0.058. So the simulator, the estimator and the reference line have no visible bias. The high estimates of the explore track come from the explore design (probable causes: in cohort 1 the explore set depends on each point's observational row, and the exploit half reacts to states that explored weeks changed). The Phase F convergence chart must say that the experimental estimate sits above the true effect.
- Phase 3: Next-week effort control in tertiles leaves confounding for an action that rises with effort. On synthetic data, forumng has no true effect, but its pooled lift (0.063) is above the quiz lift (0.039). Inside a tertile, forum use still rises with clicks, and clicks rise with ability. Diagnostics with a ceil(pct * bins) rule show finer bins reduce the forumng lift but do not remove it: 5 bins give quiz 0.030 and forumng 0.040, and 10 bins give quiz 0.031 and forumng 0.034. A probe on level 0 cells (current effort and score band in the cell) gave quiz 0.041 and forumng 0.032, but it has no interval and is not a gate result. The user chose to keep SPEC sections 7 and 10. Phase 5 net lift and the synthetic truth figure must show this result.
- Phase 5 (check before the phase): The shipped method uses level 0 cells, and it finds the true effect on synthetic data. This is a diagnostic, not a gate result. The level 0 lift tables (train points, cells with min(n_did, n_did_not) >= min_count) give a w-weighted mean lift of 0.042 for quiz (ci_low 0.026) and 0.033 for forumng. The combined se assumes independent cells, but the cells share students. On synthetic test points, LiftPath recommends quiz in 63% of its recommendations, url in 29%, glossary in 8% and forumng in 0%. The url and glossary picks are the SPEC section 20 limitation "Some winners can be chance results". The method stays as SPEC defines it.
